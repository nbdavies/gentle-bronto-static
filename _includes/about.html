About

Gentle Brontosaurus is a five-piece indie-pop band from Madison, Wisconsin, in the vein of Alvvays, The Beths, and Belle & Sebastian. Critics have called their music “effortless, breezy fun interspersed with harder-edged rockers.” Huan-Hua Chye, Nick Davies, Paul Marcou, Scott Stetson, and Anneliese Valdes each contribute vocals and instrumentation, creating intricately collaborative songs and a dynamic live show. In 2026, Gentle Brontosaurus will release their third full-length album, Three Hares.
